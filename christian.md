<div class="page-header">
  <p class="eyebrow">Faith and life</p>
  <h1>Following Christ with a steady heart.</h1>
  <p class="lead">I am a Christian and follow the Baptist teachings. This page is a simple place to gather convictions, questions, and reminders for the life of faith.</p>
</div>

<div class="prose">
  <p>Faith is not a separate compartment from the rest of life. It shapes how I think about truth, work, service, technology, and the people around me. I am still learning to live that consistently, with humility and hope.</p>

  <h2>What I hold to</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>God is the source and sustainer of all things.</li>
    <li>Jesus Christ is Lord and the centre of the Christian faith.</li>
    <li>The Bible is the trustworthy standard for faith and life.</li>
    <li>The local church is a community for worship, teaching, fellowship, and service.</li>
    <li>Grace changes how we receive forgiveness and how we extend it to others.</li>
  </ul>

  <blockquote>“The chief end of man is to glorify God, and to enjoy him for ever.”</blockquote>

  <p>For a fuller statement of the convictions I identify with, see the <a href="{{ '/confession/' | relative_url }}">Baptist Confession of Faith</a>.</p>
</div>
